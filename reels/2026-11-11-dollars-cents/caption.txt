In dollar amounts, the cents count as much as the dollars. A 10-key test checks each amount as one entry, so one cent off makes the whole amount wrong.

In the reel you can see a real minute of our dollar amounts mode. On the third entry, 70.93 was typed as 70.94 on purpose. That one entry was marked wrong, and the minute finished at 27 of 28 correct, 96.4% accuracy.

So when you hit the decimal point, slow down and check the last two digits. You can practice dollar amounts for free on our site.
