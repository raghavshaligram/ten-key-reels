Save this before your next 10-key test or data entry test.

Your score shows two speeds. Gross KPH counts every key you press, right or wrong. Net KPH only counts the right ones, so gross is always the bigger number.

In the reel you can see a real minute of our mixed mode, with one entry typed wrong on purpose. Gross came out at 10,500 KPH and net at 9,840. That gap of 660 KPH is what the one wrong entry cost, and the minute finished at 25 of 26 correct.

So when you check your result, read net first. You can practice for free on our site.
