Save this for the week before your 10-key test or data entry test.

A 1-minute test is easy to sprint, so a single score tends to flatter you a little. Our guide to the 1-minute typing test gives the fix: take three tests and note the middle result.

In the reel you can see one real minute of our mixed mode. It came out at 11,100 net KPH, 100% accuracy, 27 of 27 entries right. That's try one. Tries two and three are what turn it into a number you can trust.

So do three, write down the middle one, and practice from there.
