Save this for the week before your 10-key test or data entry test.

A 10-key score is two numbers. Speed comes out as net KPH, and accuracy is how many of your entries you got right. The test checks both. You should read both.

In the reel you can see one real minute of our mixed mode. It came out at 10,980 net KPH, with all 25 entries right, 100% accuracy.

Next time you take a practice test, write down both numbers, not just the speed. Over a week you'll see how they move together.
