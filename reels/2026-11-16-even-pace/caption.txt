Save this for the week before your 10-key test or data entry test.

You don't need faster fingers to reach 10,000 KPH. Net KPH only counts the keys you get right, so a wrong entry is time you don't get back.

In the reel you can see one real minute of our mixed mode, typed at an even pace of about 3 keys a second. It came out at 10,980 net KPH, with all 26 entries right, 100% accuracy.

Next time you practice, pick a pace you can hold for the whole minute and keep every entry clean.
