Save this if a 10-key test or a data entry test is part of a job you're applying for.

A 1-minute 10-key test is only a couple dozen entries, so one wrong entry costs you 3 or 4% of your accuracy, and once you press Enter you can't win it back. That's why rushing the last 10 seconds rarely pays.

In the reel you can see a real 1-minute run on our 10-key test with one entry typed wrong on purpose, with 7 seconds left: 900.87 went in as 910.87. The result was 23 of 24 right, 95.8% accuracy, 9,960 net KPH. That one entry is all that kept it from 100%.

Keep the same pace right to the end.
