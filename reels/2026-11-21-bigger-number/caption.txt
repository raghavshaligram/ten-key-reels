Save this if a 10-key test or a data entry test is coming up and you're not sure which number on your result is your speed.

Your result on our 10-key test shows two speeds. Gross KPH counts every key you press, right or wrong. Net KPH only counts the right ones. So when you make a mistake, gross comes out as the bigger number.

In the reel you can see a real 1-minute run with one entry typed wrong on purpose: 62.32 went in as 63.32. Gross came out at 10,920 KPH and net at 10,560, with 29 of 30 entries right.

When you check your speed, read the net.
