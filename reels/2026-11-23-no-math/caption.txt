Save this if a 10-key test or a data entry test is coming up and you're not sure what it asks you to do.

There's no math on a 10-key test. A number shows up on the screen, you type it on the number pad and press Enter, and the next one is already waiting. That keeps going until the minute is up.

In the reel you can see a real 1-minute run on our test. The first entry was 88.32. By the end it was 29 entries, none wrong, and 11,640 net KPH.

While you type, the test reminds you to keep your eyes on the numbers, not the keys. That's the habit worth practicing.
