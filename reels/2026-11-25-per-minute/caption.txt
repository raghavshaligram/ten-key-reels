Save this for the week before your 10-key test or data entry test.

10,000 KPH sounds like a lot. KPH means keys per hour, and a 1-minute test takes your minute and multiplies it by 60. So 10,000 KPH is about 167 keys in one minute.

In the reel you can see one real minute of our mixed mode. It came out at 11,100 net KPH, which is 185 right keys in that minute, with all 28 entries right.

Next time you practice, forget the big number and aim for one steady minute where every entry you type goes in right.
