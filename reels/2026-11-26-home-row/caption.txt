The number pad has a home row, the same way the letter keys do. It's 4, 5 and 6. Your index finger rests on 4, your middle finger on 5 and your ring finger on 6. The same fingers reach up for 7, 8, 9 and down for 1, 2, 3, and your thumb takes the 0.

After each number, your hand goes back to that middle row. On most keypads the 5 has a small bump, so you can find home without looking down.

Lesson 1 on our site is just the home row: one minute, and it's free with no account.
