Save this for the week before your 10-key test or data entry test.

While you type, the live test shows you a short line: "Eyes on the numbers, not the keys."

Rest your index, middle and ring fingers on 4, 5 and 6. On most keypads the 5 has a small bump, so you can feel where home is without looking. Then keep your eyes on the number and let your fingers find the keys.
